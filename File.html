About Me

Ph.D. candidate in Pharmaceutics at the University of Connecticut focused on sterile injectables, long-acting drug delivery, nanoparticle systems, and formulation science.

My research bridges bench science with regulatory strategy — developing mechanistic IVIVC models, characterizing depot formation in vivo, and generating CMC-ready datasets that inform bioequivalence submissions for long-acting injectable products.

Previously at AbbVie (Drug Product Development) and Cipla Ltd., with experience in cross-functional collaboration, technical report authoring, and scientific presentations at international conferences.

Focus Areas
Graduate Research
💉

Long-Acting Injectable Depots

Mechanistic characterization of intramuscular depot formation, in vivo stability, and drug release from complex suspension-based LAI drug products.

🧪

In Vitro Release & IVIVC

Developed novel dissolution adapter devices and mechanistic PBPK/IVIVC models supporting bioequivalence strategy.

📊

Physicochemical Characterization

Comprehensive characterization using DSC, TGA, XRPD, FTIR, DLS, SEM/TEM, and microscopy to assess CQAs and manufacturability.

🔬

Nanoparticle & LNP Systems

Long-term stability characterization of liposomal nanoparticle reference materials and UPLC-CAD analytical method development.

⚗️

Process Development & QbD

Applied DoE/QbD strategies for formulation screening, CQA definition, and process parameter optimization.

📝

Regulatory CMC & IP

CMC data package preparation, 505(b)(2) submission support, and inventor on a dissolution adapter patent licensed by PION Inc.
