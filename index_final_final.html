Focus Areas
Graduate Research
💉

Long-Acting Injectable Depots

Mechanistic characterization of intramuscular depot formation, in vivo stability, and drug release from complex suspension-based LAI drug products. Investigated agglomeration, hydration-driven transformation, and macrophage effects on dissolution.

🧪

In Vitro Release & IVIVC

Developed novel dissolution adapter devices (USP Apparatus II & IV) for LAI testing under physiologically relevant conditions. Built mechanistic PBPK/IVIVC models (GastroPlus, Phoenix WinNonlin) supporting bioequivalence strategy.

📊

Physicochemical Characterization

Comprehensive solid-state and biophysical characterization using DSC, TGA, XRPD, FTIR, DLS, SEM/TEM, and microscopy to assess CQAs, polymorphic stability, colloidal behavior, and manufacturability.

🔬

Nanoparticle & LNP Systems

18-month long-term stability characterization of liposomal nanoparticle reference materials across 7 formulation variants. Developed UPLC-CAD analytical methods for lipid degradation pathway analysis.

⚗️

Process Development & QbD

Applied DoE/QbD strategies for systematic formulation screening, critical quality attribute definition, and process parameter optimization. Generated regulatory-ready CMC datasets across multiple sterile parenteral programs.

📝

Regulatory CMC & IP

FDA-funded BAA program (contract reporting), CMC data package preparation, 505(b)(2) submission support. Inventor of U.S. Patent 63/464,677 — dissolution adapter device licensed and commercialized by PION Inc.
